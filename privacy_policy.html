Privacy Policy for Noto

Last updated: August 24, 2026
Developer: Xevon Labs (com.xevonlabs.noto)
Contact: support@xevonlabs.com

1. Overview & Core Philosophy

At Xevon Labs, we believe your personal notes, thoughts, and ideas should remain private and entirely under your control. Noto is built as an offline-first application. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or scan your personal content.

2. Information We Process

A. Your Notes and Content (100% Offline)

• Local Storage: All note titles, bodies, color themes, creation timestamps, and reminder schedules are stored exclusively on your local device using an isolated SQLite database.

• Zero Cloud Scanning: Your notes are never transmitted to our servers or any third-party cloud infrastructure.

B. Anonymous Technical Telemetry

To ensure app stability and monitor adoption, Noto transmits minimal, non-personally identifiable telemetry to our secure Supabase backend:

  • Installation Identifier: A randomly generated UUID (v4) created upon first launch (never linked to personal info).
  • Coarse Locale / Country Code: Derived from OS locale (e.g. US, PK) with zero GPS access.
  • Device Platform: Operating system name (e.g. Android, iOS).
  • Heartbeat Timestamp: Timestamp when the app is actively in use.

3. Device Permissions

Permission Technical Name Purpose
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Delivers local reminder alerts that you explicitly schedule for specific notes.
Exact Alarms SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM Ensures reminder notifications fire at the exact chosen minute even in Doze mode.
Reboot Rescheduling RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED Automatically restores scheduled local alarms after your phone restarts.
Vibration VIBRATE Provides tactile vibration feedback on button taps and alarm notifications.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

You can delete individual notes at any time, or clear all app data / uninstall the app to instantly and permanently erase all local notes.

5. Children's Privacy

Noto does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The application contains no age-restricted content.
